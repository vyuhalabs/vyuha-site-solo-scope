---
question: How do I get access?
order: 3
---

Access is by request. [Open an issue from the access-request template](https://github.com/vyuhalabs/vyuha-site-solo-scope/issues/new?template=access-request.md) and say what you want agents to work on. The issue is public, so leave out personal contact details; the reply comes on the issue. Once you have access, the [getting-started guide](/vyuha-site-solo-scope/guide/) takes you through the first steps.
