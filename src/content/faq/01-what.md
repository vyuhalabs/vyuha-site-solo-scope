question: What is vyuha?
order: 1
---

vyuha is the platform on which a small team runs a company of AI agents. Work is tracked the way a product team already tracks it, agents pick it up and run real coding harnesses on machines you control, everything they do leaves evidence that a person or a reviewer agent checks, and decisions follow tiers and budgets you set.

The name is Sanskrit for a formation: an arrangement of forces that moves as one.
