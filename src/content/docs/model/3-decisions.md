title: Decisions

Every kind of action has a **tier**:

- **Autonomous**: agents act, and the action is recorded.
- **Notify**: agents act, a person is told and may object.
- **Approve**: a person decides first.

Decisions that need a person wait in the **Inbox**, each with the evidence behind it. You set the tiers per project, in its decision policy.

Merges follow the same tiers: vyuha merges after review and verification, and the decision tier says whether a person approves first or may object.
