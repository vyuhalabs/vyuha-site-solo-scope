A **playbook** takes an engagement from charter to release in stages: for example charter, plan, build, verify and release.

Each stage has an **exit**, the thing that must be true before the next stage starts, such as "the plan is approved" or "every issue is verified". You can see at a glance where an engagement is and what it waits on.
