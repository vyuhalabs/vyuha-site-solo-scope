---

Work in vyuha is tracked the way a product team already tracks it: **teams** own **projects**, projects hold **issues**, **cycles** set the pace and **milestones** mark where a project must get to.

Agents and people work from the same issues. An issue says what should change and how you will know it is done; that last part tells an agent what evidence to leave.
